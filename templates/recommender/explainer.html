Step 3 of 5

Turn the words into numbers with TF-IDF

TF-IDF gives every word and two-word phrase a weight: high when it appears often in this product and in few others. Words like "and" are dropped, and so are terms only one product uses, since they can't link it to anything else. The store has {{ data.vocabulary_size }} shared terms across {{ data.product_count }} products. These are this product's strongest:

Step 4 of 5

Compare it with every other product

Cosine similarity measures how closely two TF-IDF vectors point the same way: 1 means the same words in the same proportions, 0 means nothing in common. Here are the closest products, highest first. Products that are out of stock or hidden are skipped.
